01

Death date from survival status

Reject 1 row · metabric OS_STATUS → death.death_date
Source
1:DECEASED 1,144 · 0:LIVING 837 · blank 528
Blocker
METABRIC contains no calendar date of any kind. The only temporal fields are AGE_AT_DIAGNOSIS, OS_MONTHS and RFS_MONTHS, all intervals. There is no anchor date to add months to, so a death date cannot be constructed at all, only invented.
Recommend
Leave DEATH empty and record survival months as an observation instead, where an interval is an honest representation. Change the row's status to note the decision rather than leaving it open.
Effect
No change to row counts. The empty death table stops looking like a defect and becomes a recorded choice.

05

METABRIC tumour stage stays a number

Reopened, then approved 1 row · metabric TUMOR_STAGE → observation.value_as_concept_id
Source
2 → 979 · NA → 721 · 1 → 630 · 3 → 144 · 0 → 24 · 4 → 11
Problem
These are bare integers with no substage and no stated AJCC edition. Your vocabulary carries edition-specific concepts, 6th, 7th and 8th, and they are not interchangeable. Choosing one would assert an edition the source never recorded.
Outcome
The objection turned out to be avoidable. Generic Stage 0-4 concepts name the stage without claiming a system, an edition, or a clinical-versus-pathological reading, none of which METABRIC states. Recommending rejection had confused "we cannot assert AJCC" with "we cannot code this at all".
Effect
1,788 observations gained a value concept; the 721 NA rows stay unmapped.
06

TCGA stage: which AJCC edition?

Your call 1 row · tcga AJCC_PATHOLOGIC_TUMOR_STAGE
Source
IIA 355 · IIB 255 · IIIA 155 · I 89 · IA 86 · IIIC 64 · IIIB 28 · IV 19 · X 14 · II 6 · IB 6 · III 2
Candidates
1635381 AJCC/UICC 7th pathological Stage 2A 1634890 AJCC/UICC 8th pathological Stage 2A 1634960 AJCC/UICC pathological Stage 2A · edition-neutral
Why you decide
Unlike METABRIC, TCGA names real substages, so these do map cleanly. The only question is edition. TCGA-BRCA cases were staged during the years the 7th edition was current, which argues for the 7th. The 8th edition changed breast staging substantially by adding biomarkers, so labelling this cohort as 8th would be wrong. The edition-neutral concepts are the cautious option if you would rather not assert one.
Also
The 14 patients recorded as STAGE X are explicitly unstageable and should stay unmapped.
Effect
1,065 of 1,079 observations gain a stage concept.

What happened after the decisions

  1. Each approved decision becomes rows in seeds/concept_map.csv with review_status changed from REVIEW to STANDARD and the concept ID filled in.
  2. Rejected decisions get their status closed with the reason recorded, so they read as settled rather than forgotten.
  3. The models that read value maps need small edits, since grade and receptor status move from a numeric slot to a concept slot.
  4. A rerun shows the effect, and the reconciliation analysis reports how far the unmapped counts fell.

Two follow-ups came out of the decisions themselves. Coding a value does not code the observation, so the four observation types needed concepts of their own before reconciliation stopped reporting 8,077 unmapped rows. And decision 1 left survival data stranded in RAW until it was carried into OBSERVATION as survival time in months.
